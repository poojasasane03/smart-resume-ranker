How it works

Three simple steps.

1

Create a job

Add the job title, required skills, optional skills and minimum experience.

2

Upload resumes

Drop in your candidates' resumes. We extract skills, education and experience.

3

Review the ranking

Get a ranked list with matched skills, gaps and insights to guide your decision.

Built to assist, not to decide

Rankings never use age, gender, religion or photographs. The tool supports your judgement: it never rejects a candidate automatically, and you can always see how a score was calculated.
